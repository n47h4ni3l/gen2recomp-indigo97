# INDIGO//97 palette guide

Version 1.5 uses individually authored three-tone material palettes. The
cartridge supplies transparency and three visible shade levels; INDIGO//97 maps
those levels to hair, skin, clothing and accent colours without changing any
opaque pixel or frame boundary.

## Player designs

- Boy: red-and-white cap, black hair, blue jacket, green backpack/gloves, pale
  trousers and red trainers.
- Crystal heroine: orange hair, yellow top, red accents, denim shorts, red
  shoes and a teal backpack.

## Johto story cast

- Silver: red hair and a charcoal outfit.
- Oak and Elm: distinct hair, shirt and trouser colours beneath pale lab coats.
- Falkner through Clair: palettes keyed to their original-series anime outfits.
- Will, Koga, Bruno, Karen and Lance: Elite Four/Champion palette set with
  Lance's red hair and cape kept prominent in rear and side views.
- Team Rocket: black uniforms, white gloves/boots and a red chest mark.
- Kimono Girls: black hair, red kimono and green obi.

## Kanto return cast

Red, Blue and all eight returning Gym Leaders use their late-1990s television
palette cues, simplified only where a 16-pixel sprite cannot hold the original
costume detail.

## Recurring services

- Nurse Joy: pink hair and dress, white cap/apron, red cap mark.
- Poké Mart clerk: green-and-cream GSC-era uniform, gold badge, navy trousers.

The clerk is period-authentic to the games rather than presented as a specific
anime character, because the original series did not give every Poké Mart a
single recurring named owner design.
